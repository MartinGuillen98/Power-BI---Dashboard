# 🎯 Business Objective

The main objective of this project was to analyze Adidas sales performance across the United States and identify the factors associated with higher sales volumes. Through exploratory data analysis and interactive visualizations, the project seeks to transform raw sales data into actionable business insights.

---

# 📂 Dataset

**Source**

(https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ahmedabbas757/dataset)

**Dataset Description**

The dataset contains sales transactions across multiple U.S. states and cities, including information about:

- Sales
- Units Sold
- Operating Profit
- Profit Margin
- Product Category
- Retailer
- Sales Method
- Geographic Location
- Invoice Date

**Why I chose this dataset**

I selected this dataset because of its clean structure, realistic business scenario, and my familiarity with the sports apparel industry. This allowed me to focus on the analytical process while generating meaningful business insights.

# 🛠 Methodology

### Data Understanding

- Reviewed the dataset structure and variables.
- Identified the main business metrics and dimensions.
- Defined the business questions to be answered.

### Data Preparation

- Validated data consistency.
- Reviewed data types and formatting.
- Prepared the dataset for visualization in Power BI.

### Exploratory Data Analysis

Performed an exploratory analysis to identify trends and relationships between:

- Sales by state and city
- Product performance
- Retailers
- Sales methods
- Profitability
- Geographic distribution

### Dashboard Development

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard featuring:

- KPI Cards
- Geographic Map
- Sales Trends
- Product Analysis
- Retailer Analysis
- Interactive Filters

### Hypothesis Validation

The analysis confirmed the initial hypothesis:

> **Adidas sales are significantly higher in coastal states and gradually decrease as the analysis moves toward the interior of the United States.**

To support this finding, additional contextual information regarding population density, income levels, tourism, and sports culture was incorporated into the analysis.

---

# 💡 Key Insights

- Coastal states generated the highest sales volumes.
- New York, San Francisco, Miami, and Charleston were the top-performing cities.
- Population density appears to positively correlate with sales performance.
- Higher-income regions generally exhibit stronger sales.
- Tourism and sports culture help explain regional sales differences.
